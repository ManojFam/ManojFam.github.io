About me

Half teacher, half fixer

From Kathmandu to Melbourne, from fixing office PCs to teaching the Diploma of IT.

I grew up in Nepal and studied Computational Mathematics at Kathmandu University. My first IT job was at an education consultancy in Kathmandu, looking after the computers, network and backups for staff and students. I also spent a few months as a back-end developer intern, writing Python and Django APIs.

I came to Melbourne for a Master of IT in Artificial Intelligence at La Trobe University. In 2025 I joined Brighton College as an IT intern. Today I work there in two roles. As IT Support Officer I look after Microsoft 365, Azure and Entra ID accounts, Moodle, Windows machines and classroom tech. As IT Trainer I teach units from the Diploma and Advanced Diploma of Information Technology.

Doing both jobs has taught me one thing: a fix only really counts when the next person understands it. So I write clear guides, explain things in plain words, and when a task keeps coming back, I automate it.
